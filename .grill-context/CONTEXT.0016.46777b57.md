~ Dormant
  An open Topic that has no live Sessions, for example after a tmux restart. It stays Dormant in the Sidebar until the user goes to it, which Reopens it, or Settles it.
  was: An open Topic that has no live Sessions, for example after a tmux restart. It stays in the Sidebar until you reopen or Settle it.
  avoid: inactive, sleeping, suspended

~ Reopen
  To bring a Settled or Dormant Topic back and create its remembered Sessions again, with their Windows, Panes, Tabs and Agents.
  was: To bring a Settled or Dormant Topic back and create its remembered Sessions again, with their Windows, Tabs and Agents.
  avoid: restore, unsettle, resume
