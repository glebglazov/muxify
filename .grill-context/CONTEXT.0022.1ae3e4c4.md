~ Snooze
  To hide one or more open Topics until a time, or until the user Unsnoozes them. Their Sessions and Agents keep running, but their Agents show no Notification until they come back. A Snooze on a Topic that is already Snoozed replaces its time.
  was: To hide an open Topic until a time, or until you unsnooze it. Its Sessions and Agents keep running, but its Agents show no Notification until it comes back.
  avoid: hide, park, defer, archive, mass snooze
